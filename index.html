Revising math papers with an AI agent

A researcher asks a coding agent to change a LaTeX paper. The tool shows what the change affects and keeps track of the decisions still open.

What the demo showed

  • See what a change affects

    After one lemma changes, the tool lists the results whose proofs use that lemma, directly or through another result. Mentions of the lemma in the prose are listed separately.

  • Change only what the author approved

    Only the approved lemma and its proof changed. The rest of the paper stayed as it was.

  • Keep open decisions with the paper

    In a new conversation, the agent still had the open author decision and the next task.

The tool does not check that the mathematics is correct. The author makes every mathematical decision.
